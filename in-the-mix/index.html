In the Mix

A private app for the DJ Paranoid Androidz. It runs on one computer on a home network and has a single account. It is not a public service: nobody else can sign up or sign in.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar (read-only) to see upcoming DJ streams and gigs.
  • Keeps the owner's Twitch stream schedule in step with that calendar.
  • Keeps an archive of past streams and mixes: recordings, tracklists and where each set was published.
  • Runs checks before a stream so the owner knows everything is ready.

Google data

In the Mix asks Google for read-only access to calendar events, and only from the owner's own account. It never changes or deletes anything in Google Calendar. The privacy policy explains what it reads, where it keeps it and how to remove access.
